# Legal

First passes, redlines, the signed index, DSRs, and diligence at 11pm.

## Agents

- [Contract Reviewer](contract-reviewer.md) — Gives you a first pass on anything that lands in your inbox · uses `email, Google Drive, Slack`.
- [First-Pass Redliner](first-pass-redliner.md) — Marks every inbound contract against your playbook and calls out what actually matters · uses `Google Drive, Notion, email`.
- [Contract Librarian](contract-librarian.md) — Every signed agreement, its terms, and its renewal — answerable by the whole team · uses `Google Drive, Slack, Notion`.
- [Template Keeper](template-keeper.md) — Versions them, and nudges when a negotiated change should become the default · uses `Google Drive, Notion, Slack`.
- [Marketing Checker](marketing-checker.md) — “Can we say this?” answered with the clause it depends on · uses `Notion, Slack, Google Docs`.
- [Subprocessor List](subprocessor-list.md) — Notices the day engineering adds a vendor · uses `Notion, GitHub, Slack`.
- [Privacy Auditor](privacy-auditor.md) — Keeps the policy matching what the product actually collects · uses `GitHub, cloud browser, Notion`.
- [DSR Handler](dsr-handler.md) — Answered inside the legal window, without a fire drill · uses `Postgres, email, Linear`.
- [Diligence Desk](diligence-desk.md) — Answers straight from the documents, at 11pm, during a raise · uses `Google Drive, email, Slack`.
- [Renewal Watcher](renewal-watcher.md) — Every auto-renew inside 60 days, flagged before it fires · uses `Google Drive, Google Calendar, email`.
- [Redline Responder](redline-responder.md) — First draft back in your standard tone · uses `Google Drive, email, Notion`.
