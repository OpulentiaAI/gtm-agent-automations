# Sales

CRM hygiene, outbound, trials, renewals, and the brief before you walk into the room.

## Agents

- [SDR](sdr.md) — Maintains a CRM that stays current on its own, from Granola call notes, email, and a quick Slack ping · uses `Granola, HubSpot, Slack`.
- [Lead Qualifier](lead-qualifier.md) — Researches and scores every inbound signup, and puts the ten worth your time on your calendar · uses `HubSpot, Google Calendar, email`.
- [Follow-Up](sales-follow-up.md) — The recap and the one thing you promised, sent within an hour of hanging up · uses `Gong, HubSpot, email`.
- [Salesforce Oracle](salesforce-oracle.md) — End-of-quarter question answered on your phone in 10 seconds, no laptop · uses `Salesforce, text, Slack`.
- [Prospector](prospector.md) — Finds companies hiring for the roles you replace, with a first line you’d actually send · uses `LinkedIn, cloud browser, email`.
- [Outbound](outbound.md) — Writes in your own voice, prioritizes leads, and coordinates follow-up across email and LinkedIn · uses `email, LinkedIn, HubSpot`.
- [Account Tierer](account-tierer.md) — Sorts every account by fit and warmth, and enriches the contacts in batches · uses `Postgres, HubSpot, Google Sheets`.
- [Churn Early Warning](churn-early-warning.md) — Finds the account whose usage is dropping while the relationship looks fine · uses `Salesforce, Amplitude, Slack`.
- [Call Coach](call-coach.md) — Timestamped comments on your own calls and homework before the next one · uses `Gong, Slack, Notion`.
- [Win/Loss Analyst](win-loss-analyst.md) — Turns a week of calls into a memo with the phrases that actually closed · uses `Gong, Notion, Slack`.
- [Deal Cartographer](deal-cartographer.md) — Maps who really influences it, from LinkedIn and the CRM · uses `LinkedIn, Salesforce, Notion`.
- [Localizer](sales-localizer.md) — Has the deck in the customer’s language the night before the call · uses `Google Drive, Google Docs, email`.
- [Deck Updater](deck-updater.md) — Rewrites it live from call notes and sends a custom deck after the call · uses `Granola, Google Drive, email`.
- [Questionnaire Filler](questionnaire-filler.md) — Drafts from your public docs and leaves only the human questions · uses `Notion, Google Sheets, email`.
- [Report Builder](report-builder.md) — Makes the dashboard once, so nobody asks you twice. Ping it when you need a new one · uses `Salesforce, Slack`.
- [Meeting Brief](meeting-brief.md) — Everyone you’re seeing today researched, including how they use the product, informed by your analytics · uses `Google Calendar, Amplitude, text`.
- [Champion Tracker](champion-tracker.md) — Tells you the day yours changes jobs · uses `LinkedIn, HubSpot, Slack`.
- [Battlecard Keeper](battlecard-keeper.md) — Updates it from what you actually lose on · uses `Gong, Notion, Slack`.
- [Trial Runner](trial-runner.md) — Works day 2, day 7, and day 13, and hands you a list of who to call · uses `Amplitude, email, HubSpot`.
- [MAP Writer](map-writer.md) — After every enterprise call, with the owners chased · uses `Notion, email, Slack`.
- [Renewal Builder](renewal-builder.md) — Makes the case from usage data, 60 days before the date · uses `Postgres, HubSpot, Slack`.
