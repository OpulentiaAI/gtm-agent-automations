# Founders

Operator agents for a founder desk: email and calendar, inbound, close-the-loop, burn, intel, and a chief of staff.

## Agents

- [Executive Assistant](executive-assistant.md) — Runs your email and your calendar, and can schedule you from email, Slack, or iMessage · uses `email, Google Calendar, Slack, text`.
- [Inbound Sales](inbound-sales.md) — Researches and enriches every signup, prioritizes the top, and drafts a personalized note · uses `Attio or HubSpot, prod db, email`.
- [Meeting Closer](meeting-closer.md) — Sends the notes, opens the tickets and action items, and drafts the follow-up email the way you like — internal and external · uses `Granola, Linear, email`.
- [Burn Analyzer](burn-analyzer.md) — Tracks actual spend, reconciles invoices from email, and helps you know the numbers cold before the books close · uses `Mercury, email, Ramp`.
- [Calendar Defender](calendar-defender.md) — Color-codes and protects your time against your priorities, finds focus time, and declines the junk · uses `Google Calendar, Slack, text`.
- [Inbox Triager](inbox-triager.md) — Hourly, brings you back down to what you actually have to decide or respond to personally · uses `email, text, Slack`.
- [Person Who Knows](person-who-knows.md) — Answers anyone's “how does this work?” with a link, from your docs, Slack, and repos · uses `Notion, Slack, GitHub`.
- [Morning Digest](morning-digest.md) — Slack, email, calendar, and call notes — with your todos runnable from a DM · uses `Slack, email, Google Calendar, text`.
- [Podcast Booker](podcast-booker.md) — Reaches out to relevant podcasters or guests in your niche automatically each week, then books them · uses `email, cloud browser, Google Calendar`.
- [Automation Scout](automation-scout.md) — Reads your Slack, Notion, GitHub, and Linear history and proposes two automations, unprompted · uses `Slack, Notion, GitHub, Linear`.
- [Chief of Staff](chief-of-staff.md) — Routes work to the right specialist agent and coordinates your agent team so you don't have to · uses `Slack, text, Linear`.
- [Meeting Prepper](meeting-prepper.md) — Who, why, what happened last time, and what you want out of it · uses `Google Calendar, HubSpot, Notion`.
- [Investment Memos](investment-memos.md) — Tracks the month as it happens and live-updates the memo so you are always ready to press send · uses `Google Drive, Postgres, Granola`.
- [Promise Tracker](promise-tracker.md) — Everything you said in public — helps you close things · uses `Slack, Notion, Granola`.
- [Landing Page Tester](landing-page-tester.md) — Creates new landing pages, runs Meta ads, and tests conversion · uses `Vercel, cloud browser, Amplitude`.
- [Head of Content](head-of-content.md) — Helps you maintain your socials, stay up to date, and respond fast in DMs · uses `X, LinkedIn, text`.
- [Sales Double](sales-double.md) — Monitors every channel you are reachable on, makes sure you respond fast, and reprioritizes · uses `Slack, email, text, Attio`.
